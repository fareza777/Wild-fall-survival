Wild Fall: Survival RPG
Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Wild Fall: Survival RPG (“the game”) is an offline wilderness survival game published by F7 Developer. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the Android app.

Information stored on your device

Your journey, settings, accessibility preferences, local statistics, and optional ad-reward state are stored on your device. The game does not require an account and does not send your saved game or journal entries to our servers. Uninstalling the app may remove local data.

Advertising

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for an optional banner, occasional interstitials, and an optional rewarded supply pack. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interaction information to provide, measure, and protect advertising, subject to your device settings and Google’s policies. You can choose not to watch a rewarded ad. See Google’s advertising policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google Play purchases

The game offers an optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the purchase, payment, entitlement, and license information. We do not receive or store your payment card number. The product is non-consumable and removes banner and interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain optional.

Audio and permissions

The game plays bundled nature ambience, physical sound effects, and optional spoken story narration locally. It does not record microphone, camera, contacts, or precise location. Android network access is used by Google Play services and advertising when those features are available.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above will be updated with material changes. For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the public project page at github.com/fareza777/Wild-fall-survival.
