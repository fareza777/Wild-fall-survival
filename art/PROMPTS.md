# WILDFALL — Ash & Copper art

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Original PNGs are preserved in generated/. Runtime WebP assets are resized and format-converted only. No semantic image manipulation was applied.

The full per-asset prompt, destination, size, and transparency specification is in [catalog.json](catalog.json). Art direction: obsidian, slate, ivory, and ember copper, tactile painterly realism. All humans have a hood, mask, or helmet concealing the face. Camp architecture and fire are separate layers driven by the simulation.

The title illustration depicts the intended survival atmosphere; it is separate from the state-driven camp view.
